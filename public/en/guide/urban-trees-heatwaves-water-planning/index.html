How can communities support urban trees during heatwaves?

A practical guide to urban tree shade, root-zone moisture, responsible watering and checking misleading online advice.

Observe before acting

Consider the tree species, planting date, soil drainage and local water restrictions. Newly planted trees may have less established roots. Drooping leaves alone do not prove drought stress.

Help water reach the roots

When watering is appropriate, let water infiltrate the root zone slowly rather than run off the surface. Check soil moisture and avoid waterlogging. No universal daily schedule or fixed number of litres suits every tree.

Mulch and responsible care

Suitable organic mulch can help reduce evaporation; keep it away from the trunk. Do not dig, prune or modify irrigation around public trees without permission.

School activity and media literacy

With permission, compare shaded and unshaded spaces and observe soil moisture. Question posts that prescribe the same daily watering amount for all trees without considering local climate, soil, species or restrictions.
