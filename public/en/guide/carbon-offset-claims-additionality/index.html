Carbon offset claims and additionality

What does carbon neutral mean?

A flight, product or event advertised as carbon neutral has not necessarily eliminated its emissions. Some claims combine direct reductions with credits for purported reductions or removals elsewhere. The quality of the credits and the scope of the claim require separate scrutiny.

Additionality and risks

If a project would have happened in essentially the same way without credit revenue, its additional climate benefit is questionable. Credible accounting also needs sound baselines, safeguards against double counting, consideration of permanence and leakage when emissions shift elsewhere. Wildfire can affect forest carbon storage; risks must be assessed for each project.

Five questions

What emissions has the company actually reduced? Which boundaries and years are included? Are the credit standard, registry identifier and retirement record public? How are additionality and durability assessed? Does the advertisement distinguish reductions from compensation?

Classroom activity

Compare two advertisements using the terms zero emissions, net zero and offset. Record the source, date, boundaries, credit type and evidence. Mark missing information as not disclosed rather than inventing figures.
